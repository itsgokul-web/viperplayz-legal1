# VIPERPLAYZ – PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** November 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 10, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VIPERPLAYZ ("the Bot") may collect, process, store and use information when users and Discord servers interact with its services.

VIPERPLAYZ is a multipurpose Discord bot providing music, moderation, security, automation and community-management functionality.

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary to operate, maintain and protect the Bot.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled and commands used, VIPERPLAYZ may process the following categories of information.

### A. Discord Account and Server Identifiers

- Discord user IDs.
- Discord server (guild) IDs.
- Channel IDs.
- Role IDs.
- Bot and application identifiers.

These identifiers may be needed to associate commands, settings, permissions and moderation records with the appropriate Discord server.

### B. Server Configuration

Depending on enabled modules, the Bot may store:

- Welcome channel settings.
- Moderation configurations.
- Security thresholds and limits.
- Automod rules.
- Role configuration.
- Giveaway settings.
- Ignored command settings.
- Music configuration.
- Automation settings.
- Other server-specific preferences.

### C. Moderation and Security Information

If relevant features are enabled, the Bot may process:

- Warning records.
- Moderation case information.
- Ban, kick and timeout records.
- Security incidents.
- Spam and anti-raid detection events.
- Message information necessary for enabled filtering rules.
- Administrator and moderator action records.

### D. Music Information

When music functionality is used, the Bot may process:

- Track titles.
- Artist information.
- Track URLs.
- Requesting user IDs.
- Voice and text channel identifiers.
- Queue and playback state.

Music queues and playback information may be temporary unless explicitly saved by an implemented feature.

### E. Technical and Diagnostic Information

The Bot's infrastructure may generate technical logs containing:

- Error messages.
- Command execution events.
- Timestamps.
- Service connection events.
- Diagnostic information.
- Relevant Discord identifiers.

Logs are used for troubleshooting, security and service maintenance.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- Execute Discord commands.
- Provide music functionality.
- Maintain server-specific settings.
- Apply moderation rules.
- Detect spam and suspicious activity.
- Generate security alerts.
- Maintain moderation records.
- Troubleshoot technical problems.
- Prevent abuse and protect infrastructure.
- Improve service reliability.

We do not claim to use Discord information for advertising or sell personal information to advertisers.

## 4. Message Content and Filtering

Some VIPERPLAYZ features, such as automod, anti-spam and abusive-word filtering, may need to inspect message content.

Where these features are enabled, relevant message content may be processed to identify rule violations.

We aim to avoid retaining complete message contents unnecessarily.

Whether message content is temporarily or persistently stored depends on the implemented feature and its configuration.

## 5. Data Storage

VIPERPLAYZ currently uses an SQLite database in its application environment for persistent application data.

The application may also generate technical logs and temporary runtime data.

Depending on deployment and configuration, data may be stored on the infrastructure operated by the Bot's administrator.

Access should be restricted to authorized operators and systems.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose, subject to operational, security and legal requirements.

Different information categories may have different retention periods.

Examples include:

- Server settings: retained while required to provide configured services or until deleted.
- Moderation records: retained according to the configured moderation-record retention policy.
- Security logs: retained according to operational and security requirements.
- Temporary music information: generally used during active playback sessions.
- Technical diagnostic logs: retained according to the applicable logging and maintenance configuration.

We do not promise a specific number of retention days unless an explicit retention schedule has been implemented and published.

When information is no longer necessary, it should be securely deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.

## 7. Data Sharing

VIPERPLAYZ does not sell user information.

Information may be processed or transmitted where necessary to:

- Communicate with Discord's API.
- Connect to Lavalink for music playback.
- Access supported third-party audio providers.
- Operate hosting, infrastructure and technical services.
- Comply with legal obligations.
- Protect the Bot, its users and Discord communities.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and practices.

## 8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information.

These measures may include:

- Restricting access to application databases.
- Protecting authentication credentials.
- Using secure server configurations.
- Monitoring application errors.
- Maintaining backups where appropriate.
- Limiting access to authorized operators.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should never share passwords, bot tokens, authentication codes or other confidential credentials with the Bot or its operators.
